American Library Association

The ALA is the nation library association for the United States. The non-profit organization dedicates itself to the advancement of library and information services by setting industry standards and advocating for intellectural freedom

Pennsylvania Library Association

PaLA focuses specifically on libraries and information services in the state of Pennsylvania. The organization covers various types of libraries within the state and is dedicated to education and advocacy.

Society of American Archivists

SAA is a professional organization whose mission is to support professional archivists dedicated to the core values of archival work.

SAA Website

Black Caucus American Library Association

The BCALA is an organization working towards the development and improvement of library services dedicated to serving the African American community, and recruiting and supporting the needs of Black librarians.
