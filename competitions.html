Queen of the Park

An annual women's skate competition at FDR Skatepark, open to women and nonbinary skaters. The contest format is jam-style. First place prize is $1000 with a $200 prize for best trick. The competition also includes vendors and live music.

Location: FDR Skatepark

Date: June 6, 2026; 12 noon - Dark

Philly AM Skateboard Contest

The contest divisions are 13 & Under, Advanced, Womens, Intermediate, 30 & Over. The contest format is Jam style, 5 skaters out for 5 minutes with heats. Prizes for top 3 from sponsor New Balance, a $500 cash for 1st place.
